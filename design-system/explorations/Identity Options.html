LIFECYCLE

Your espresso cups, as a progression ramp

Green → red → yellow → brown. Four stages, and the app has exactly two four-stage progressions. This ramp is independent of the palette above — it pairs with any of A/B/C, and it's the one place coffee colour belongs.

Green growth · #4e7c4a
Red cherries · #a8323c
Yellow dried · #d19a2e
Brown roast · #6f4e37
Mapping 1 — bag lifecycle pills
frozenrestingactivefinished

A bag held green, developing red, ready yellow, spent brown. Reads left-to-right as the cupboard's own timeline.

Mapping 2 — session phase stepper
1Confirm
2Brew
3Post-brew
4Tasting

The stepper earns colour: each phase owns a stage, so the sub-bar shows where you are without reading it. Specialty drinks use stages 1 · 2 · 4.

Pick one mapping, or both — but not neither and not three. Two colour languages in one app is the ceiling.
